A FEW IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Evidence, with its limits intact.

Is Phoenix a production monitoring platform today?

Phoenix is at prototype stage. This site demonstrates the intended workflow using synthetic data. Connected monitoring, customer integrations and automated release controls require further development and validation.

Does a high score mean an agent is safe?

No. A result applies to the configuration, tasks and conditions tested. It does not establish universal reliability, certification or suitability for a different workflow.

How is this different from another benchmark?

The proposed service is organised around a customer's release decision: a defined workflow, realistic failure scenarios, version-specific evidence and an explicit account of what remains untested.

Where does the demo data go?

The demo runs in your browser. It uses no analytics or backend submission. Downloads are generated locally. Hosting providers may process ordinary web request logs.
